# How I buy

Snipe Hunt reads this on every run. Write it the way you'd brief a friend who is bidding for you.

- **Cheapskate snipes.** The max should be the price where winning is still a steal compared with recent sales. Use odd-cent maxes like 3.03, 6.06, 12.12 and 25.25.
- **Always count shipping.** A $4 card with $9.99 shipping isn't a steal.
- **Unique over parallels.** New photos, new designs, inserts and oddballs beat another colour of a card I already own.
- **Things to show off.** Autos, patch autos, low-numbered cards (/50 and lower), case hits, short prints, 1/1s, misspelled-title steals.
- **Combined shipping.** Batch wins from one seller and pay once on a combined invoice.
- **Skip.** Common base cards I already own, slabs from graders I don't trust, and Buy It Now listings priced well above sold prices.
- **Signed base cards aren't autos.** A BAS/JSA slabbed signature on a paper base card is worth maybe $20–30 at most. Never tier one "fire".
- **Buy It Now needs real comps.** A Buy It Now over $25 needs real comps; if the value is an estimate, say so and give it 3 stars at most.
- **Watch new sellers.** Be skeptical of sellers under about 100 feedback on anything over $30.
